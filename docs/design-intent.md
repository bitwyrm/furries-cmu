# Furries@CMU — original intent notes

This file preserves the key words and context from the original brief. The current, actionable visual direction lives in the Figma-ready [design prompt](figma.md); use it as the single design specification.

## Original keywords and vibe

- Fox-core, with brick, burnt orange, orangish red, and black as palette inspiration.
- Pixel-core and pixelated artwork.
- Retro web-core, handmade web zine, postmodern, playful, and a little unusual.
- Indie-studio character: personal, human-made, and community-centered.
- Whimsy and some interaction, while keeping the site comfortable to use on phones and laptops.

The original suggested color swatches were `#4C251E`, `#8C0101`, `#C03415`, `#D66E48`, `#E5B992`, `#F1E4D4`, and `#2468C0`. These were examples, not a fixed palette or color map.

## Club context to preserve

Furries@CMU is a welcoming place for furries, furry-adjacent people, and people curious about the fandom. A fursuit is not required. The club wants to make room for community, food, material resources, and teaching people how to make a fursuit.

The club's monthly activity is **Howling at the Moon**: a walk to Walking to the Sky followed by a movie and snacks. Dates are announced on Discord. The club has a logo, and each officer should have a picture, bio, and pronouns. Fursona material is optional. An FAQ is part of the site.
