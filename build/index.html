First things first

So, what’s
furry?

The fandom is about anthropomorphic animal characters and the people who draw, make, write, and play with them. You can be into the art, suits, hangouts, or learning about it.

Anthropomorphic: animal characters with human traits. Art, craft, stories, and community grow out from there.

FIELD NOTES / PHOTO ROLL 01 — 03

Asked before / answered plainly

Good questions.

The quick answers are already up front. Open a question if you want the longer version.

01 Do I need a fursuit? +

Nope. You can come in everyday clothes, a partial suit, a full suit, or with no plans to wear one at all.

02 I’m furry-adjacent or just curious. Can I come? +

Yes. Furries@CMU welcomes furry, furry-adjacent, and curious people who want to share a friendly space.

03 How do I join? +

Join the club’s Discord for updates, upcoming get-togethers, and each month’s Howling at the Moon date.

04 Can I come if I don’t know anyone yet? +

Yes. It is fine to come on your own. You can also check Discord beforehand if you want to know what to expect.

05 What should I bring to a meetup? +

Nothing special. Bring whatever helps you feel comfortable, and check Discord for any details about a specific meetup.

06 Can I learn fursuit-making if I’m a beginner? +

Yes. FAME is where members work on fursuits and protogens. The club stocks fur and other supplies, and members can request materials through the form.
